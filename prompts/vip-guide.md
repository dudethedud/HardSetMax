<!-- How VIP works, written from docs/SPEC.md. Keep it in sync if the settings change. -->
- **VIP** goes to the 10 most active members of the last 7 days. It's updated once a day at midnight (server time), never live.
- **Score** comes from chatting (at most one message per minute counts; spam, repeats and very short messages don't) and from time in voice with other people (5 voice minutes ≈ 1 message, up to 4 hours a day). Some channels count less, and bot-spam channels don't count.
- **Keeping VIP:** VIPs keep their slot down to rank #13, and nobody loses VIP by rank in their first 5 days. Three days with no activity, a timeout, a staff role or leaving the server removes VIP. A minimum score is needed to get VIP.
- **VIP perks:** image and embed perms, external emojis and stickers, soundboard, the private VIP Lounge (text and voice), a VIP colour from the lounge picker, VIP polls, your own temporary VIP voice channel, a spot on the public #vip-board, a private profile card (the 🪪 My profile button) and one 📣 shoutout a day in main chat (posted by the bot, no pings). VIPs can also give one 🦴 treat a day to anyone (just for fun, it doesn't change scores) and 🎨 suggest a new VIP colour for staff to approve. After 14 days in a row as VIP they're a 💎 Veteran: marked on the board, with a second daily shoutout. When you're busy, VIPs get your replies first.
- **VIP polls:** in the polls channel, each VIP can post one poll per day, and the whole server gets up to 2 VIP polls a day. The bot pings the Polls role and makes a discussion thread.
- **Crown:** each Monday the #1 VIP gets the 👑 Crown for the week: their own custom role, a 👑 in front of their name, three titles to hand out, each with a job (🛡️ Champion: a guest VIP pass; 📜 Advisor: posts VIP polls and gets a daily shoutout; 🃏 Jester: sets your status and asks you for a trick once a day), one decree a week in announcements, one pardon a week (a VIP who can't be dropped at the next reshuffle), picking your mood for the week, a daily spotlight on the VIP board, a theme of the week, one rainbow speech a week (someone's messages get rainbow flair for 10 minutes), and a voice hall with Priority Speaker. Losing VIP loses the Crown and its titles.
- **Badges:** holding VIP for 4, 12 and 26 weeks in total earns permanent badges (VIP Regular, VIP Veteran, VIP Legend).
- Admins can pin or remove VIPs. Nobody can get VIP by asking the bot.
